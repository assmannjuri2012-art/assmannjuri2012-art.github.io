Questions

Is Operation Syndicate free?

Yes. The whole game is free to play in your browser – no download, no account, no ads.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open the play page in Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a computer; the game (about 37 MB) loads and starts by itself. Your browser needs WebGL 2, which every current browser has.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, in your browser on this computer. Come back later and press Continue. Clearing the site data of your browser deletes the save.

Can I play on a phone or tablet?

The game is made for keyboard and mouse or a gamepad, so a computer is best. Phones and tablets have no touch controls.

Is there multiplayer?

Online free roam with friends is part of the desktop version. The browser version is the full single-player game.

The game runs slowly – what can I do?

Open Settings in the menu and lower the graphics quality. Closing other tabs helps too. A computer with a dedicated or recent integrated graphics chip runs it best.
